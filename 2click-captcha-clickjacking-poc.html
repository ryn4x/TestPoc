About this POC · impact and mitigations

Impact shown: a victim who thinks they are tapping two reward buttons is actually tapping the two verification points of a bank transfer. In the built-in simulation, because the coordinates are supplied in the URL as percentages, the invisible CAPTCHA targets and the visible bait stay perfectly aligned on any screen size.

Debug mode (?debug) is the attacker's setup step. Drag the two click targets, or paste a URL to frame a real target page and drag the markers onto its real controls. The generated link contains the two coordinate pairs plus the optional encoded u= target — no debug flag and no UI hints.

Framed targets: the attack link positions a transparent iframe exactly over the bait so real clicks reach the framed page. With a framed target the overlay collapses to compact icon-sized controls so it can sit on small target elements. Same-origin targets expose click progress to this POC; cross-origin targets receive the clicks but cannot be observed by the attacker page, so no proof is shown for them. Positions are percentages of the stage, so the overlay scales with the frame; a target whose controls reflow differently at another viewport may need fresh coordinates for that viewport. Targets that send X-Frame-Options / frame-ancestors or frame-bust will refuse to load or escape the frame.

Real-world requirements: the victim page must be frameable (no X-Frame-Options / CSP frame-ancestors), the CAPTCHA controls must be positionable or predictable, and the sensitive action must be reachable with two clicks. The built-in simulation demonstrates all three offline; framing a URL requires network access to that URL.

Mitigations:

  • Send X-Frame-Options: DENY and Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none'.
  • Do not accept layout or CAPTCHA coordinates from URL parameters; derive them server-side.
  • Validate the CAPTCHA token server-side and make it single-use.
  • Require a deliberate, visible confirmation for sensitive actions; never authorize on two blind clicks.
  • Use frame-busting as defence in depth, not as the only control.

Built-in pages are simulated. Framing a URL loads only the URL you enter. No real bank, no data collection. For authorized security testing and awareness demonstrations.
